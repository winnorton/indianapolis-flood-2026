# WS1 — Evidence & Records (everyone)

**Goal:** every dollar of damage is provable later, even though the house may already be
gutted. Evidence is the foundation under the insurance claim, FEMA award, SBA loan, the
substantial-damage decision, and any appeal.

**Agent note:** if tear-out happened before the adjuster inspected (`TEAROUT_DONE` = yes,
`INSPECTED` = no), say plainly: "Your photos and receipts now ARE the claim. Let's secure
them today." Use `templates/EVIDENCE_INDEX_TEMPLATE.md` as the checklist.

## Steps

1. **Name a secure home for evidence** (cloud folder, external drive, or physical binder).
   *Done when:* the user tells you where it is. Never suggest anywhere public.
2. **Gather pre-tear-out documentation:** photos/videos, water-line marks, drywall cut
   height, appliance model plates, a wide shot per room.
   *Done when:* indexed per the template, originals preserved with dates/metadata.
3. **Collect the remediation firm's records** — daily drying logs, final material moisture
   readings, scope of work, invoices, disposal receipts. Firms demobilize and become hard
   to reach; request in writing now.
   *Done when:* documents received or a dated written request is on record.
4. **Keep material samples** where possible (flooring piece, drywall section).
5. **Start the receipts habit:** every purchase and payment gets one category — building
   repair / contents / temporary housing / mitigation / professional fees. One expense,
   one category, one payer (this prevents duplication-of-benefits debt later).
6. **Write the timeline narrative:** dated first-person chronology from the flood to today.
   *Done when:* saved in the secure location; update weekly.

## Stop / escalate

- Missing evidence for something significant → don't recreate or stage anything; write a
  gap note and get a contractor/witness statement instead.
- Anyone asks the user to sign away rights to access their documentation → licensed advice
  first.
