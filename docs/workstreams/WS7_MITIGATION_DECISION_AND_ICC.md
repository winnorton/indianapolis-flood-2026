# WS7 — Mitigation Decision & ICC (major damage)

**Goal:** the user picks their path — elevate / demolish-and-rebuild / repair /
express buyout interest — with real numbers, and (if insured and substantially damaged)
captures the $30,000 ICC benefit correctly.

**Do not open this decision until WS5 has produced:** the written determination, the
written BFE/floodway answer, and the appraisal. Deciding earlier wastes money.

## Steps

1. **Get three comparable bids** from one written scope (elevation priced separately from
   foundation, utilities, access, engineering, permits, contingency). Never compare lump
   sums. Verify license and insurance; beware large upfront deposits.
2. **Fill the funding worksheet** (`templates/FUNDING_WORKSHEET.md`): the 50% ratio, the
   insurance cap math, and the project ledger. Every number needs a source document;
   "unknown" is an acceptable entry, a guess is not.
3. **Understand the funding gap honestly:** elevation typically runs $90k–$150k+. ICC pays
   at most $30k (insured + substantially damaged only). The SBA **mitigation increase**
   (+20% of verified damage, request within 2 years of loan approval, elevation explicitly
   eligible) is the main gap-filler; ask SBA for it in writing when bids exist.
4. **ICC claim mechanics (insured):** separate claim from the building claim. Four
   documents: the qualifying SD letter (WS5 step 4), the permit specifying the required
   mitigation, the signed contractor contract, the ICC Proof of Loss. Ask the carrier
   about an advance (historically up to half / $15k) — amount and conditions in writing.
   Final ICC payment releases only on the certificate of occupancy/compliance letter.
   **Cap warning:** building payments + ICC together can't exceed the building coverage
   limit — model before final settlement (WS3 step 5).
5. **Buyout/acquisition interest:** voluntary, city-initiated, slow, and NOT currently a
   declared program for this event — treat as contingent. Expressing interest to DBNS/DPW
   costs nothing; never delay real recovery waiting for it, and never budget grant money
   without a written funded offer naming the property.
6. **Write a one-page decision memo:** chosen path, why it complies, total cost, confirmed
   funding, accepted self-funded gap, schedule, go/no-go condition. *Done when:* signed
   and saved.

## Stop / escalate

- Any contract signed against hoped-for grants → stop; confirmed money only.
- Floodway complications, unusual foundations, or historic structures → engineer first.
- The mitigation math genuinely doesn't work → revisit WS4 step 5 / buyout interest, and
  discuss long-term options honestly rather than pushing a path.
