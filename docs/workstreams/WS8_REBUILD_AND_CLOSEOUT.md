# WS8 — Rebuild & Closeout (everyone rebuilding; open last)

**Goal:** construction happens under permit, on budget, with the paper trail that releases
final payments and lowers future risk and premiums.

## Steps

1. **Contract properly:** scope matches the permit (and the ICC submission if applicable);
   explicit price, allowances, change-order process, schedule, insurance certificates,
   lien waivers, payment milestones tied to completed work. Professional review scaled to
   contract size. *Done when:* signed contract + permits + notice to proceed.
2. **Control changes:** no change order proceeds without written scope, price, schedule
   effect, permit effect, and payer category. Reconcile each against the funding worksheet.
3. **Inspections and closeout:** DBNS final inspection → **certificate of occupancy /
   compliance letter** (this releases the final ICC payment for insured SD homes) → final
   photos, paid invoices, lien waivers, warranties into the evidence binder.
4. **New Elevation Certificate** (if elevated): order it post-construction. Submitting it
   to the insurer is voluntary and can only lower the premium.
5. **Insurance reset:** get a written premium quote using the new elevation/compliance
   data; keep flood insurance in force — after federal flood assistance in the floodplain
   it's a condition of future help, and Indianapolis's CRS discount applies.
6. **Final money reconciliation:** the funding worksheet balances to zero unexplained
   difference; every source (insurance, FEMA, SBA, ICC, state, own funds) maps to
   categorized expenses. Then file the final status-log entry: recovery closed.

## Stop / escalate

- Contractor demands large cash upfront, has no license/insurance, or pressures same-day
  signing → walk away; report fraud to the Indiana Attorney General.
- Work diverging from permit → pause and resolve with DBNS before continuing; unpermitted
  work can void ICC and future insurability.
- Punch-list disputes at the end → withhold only contractually allowed retainage; get the
  list in writing.
