White River Flood Recovery Guide — August 2026

A community recovery playbook for Indianapolis homeowners affected by the August 2026 White River flood (FEMA DR-4933-IN).

First three things, in order:

  1. Are you safe and housed tonight? If not, call 211 first.
  2. Apply to FEMA (DR-4933-IN, deadline Oct 25) and SBA (IN-20022, deadline Oct 26). Then log in to DisasterAssistance.gov the same day — letters with 30-day deadlines post within minutes.
  3. If you have flood insurance, get your date of loss and Proof of Loss deadline in writing. It is 60 days from the date of loss.
