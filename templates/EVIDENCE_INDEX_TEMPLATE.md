**Keep the filled-in index and the evidence itself in your secure location — never public.**
If tear-out happened before the adjuster inspected, this index effectively *is* your claim.
Preserve originals and file metadata; never recreate or alter evidence. Where something is
missing, write a gap entry and get a contractor/witness statement instead.
